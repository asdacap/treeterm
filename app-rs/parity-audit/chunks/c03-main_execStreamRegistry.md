# c03-main_execStreamRegistry

Verified: +1 rows, 9 rows changed

Architecture note: Electron keeps every in-flight exec in a main-process registry keyed by a
renderer-visible `execId`, because the gRPC stream (main) and the awaiting promise (renderer)
live in different processes. The port has one process: `ExecClient::exec` hands the caller an
`ExecProcess` whose `events` stream carries the output and exactly one terminal item; dropping
it cancels the call (and the daemon kills the command). So the registry CRUD is NA plumbing;
the capabilities behind it (cancel, settle on loss, one terminal event) are mapped per row.

(v) Second-audit summary: the failAllForConnection gap is real but narrower than "every exec
on a lost connection". The port's channels use a single-use connector
(app-rs/crates/treeterm-core/src/transport.rs:63), so any exec whose socket actually closes
(local daemon exit/restart, ssh killed by DropTransport on Reconnecting) fails at once with
`ExecError::Api`. What is left is a connection that goes Reconnecting while its socket stays
open (local heartbeat timeout on a wedged or silent daemon): an exec whose owner outlives the
generation (e.g. the detached branch-listing task in app-rs/crates/treeterm-app/src/child_dialog.rs:199)
waits for its backstop instead of failing at once, and the "connection ... lost while command
was running" text never appears.
