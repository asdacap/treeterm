# c62-renderer_store_createTrafficMeterStore

Verified: +1 rows, 7 rows changed

Electron: one window-wide zustand meter (`createTrafficMeterStore`) keyed by workspace id and connection id, ticking every second. It outlives session stores (`src/renderer/store/app.ts:101`: "a reconnect rebuild keeps the counts"). Port: `treeterm-core/src/traffic.rs` (constants, `ByteCounter`, `RateWindow`, `TrafficRate`), plus one `treeterm-model/src/traffic.rs` `TrafficMeter` entity per counter. The connection's meter is owned by `SessionStore` and fed by `DaemonConnection::received()`. Each workspace's meter is owned by `WorkspaceStore` and fed by its terminals' `PtyEvent::Data`. Both are shown by `treeterm-app/src/traffic_badge.rs`.
