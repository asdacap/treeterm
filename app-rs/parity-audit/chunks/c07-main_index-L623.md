Architecture note: Electron main keeps one gRPC client per window and forwards events to renderers over IPC. The port is one process: `AppStore` (treeterm-model/src/app_store.rs) owns the `ConnectionManager` and, for each `Connected` status, builds a new `SessionStore` on that connection generation. That store owns its session watch, workspace file watches and the lock channel (`DaemonConnection.session`). On every later status change the store is disposed and its views are dropped. So Electron's "re-establish watches after reconnect" and "settle execs on connection loss" logic becomes "rebuild or tear down the generation".
